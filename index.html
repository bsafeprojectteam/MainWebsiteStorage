We collected water samples from the lake and took them to the laboratory for analysis. This step was an important part of helping students understand how pollution affects water quality and the ecosystem. We are now waiting for the test results.


According to the analysis results of the water sample we collected from Uşak Karaağaç Lake, the presence of Enterococcus and Streptococcus bacteria was detected. These microorganisms are naturally found in the environment as well as in the human microbiota. Through this study, our students had the opportunity to better understand how microorganisms can be present even in natural water sources and why regular monitoring of environmental water quality is important for public health and ecological awareness. This experience helped them develop scientific thinking skills and increased their awareness of environmental hygiene.


×

We sterilized the water sample collected from the lake using the pressurized steam sterilization method (autoclaving). This is the most commonly used sterilization technique in laboratories. During the process, the liquid is exposed to high temperature and high pressure steam. Typically, materials are kept at 121°C for 15 minutes inside the autoclave. At this temperature, both microorganisms and their spores are effectively destroyed. Autoclaving is considered one of the most reliable sterilization methods, although it requires a specialized device. By applying this technique, we ensured that the lake water sample was completely sterilized and safe for further laboratory analysis and educational demonstrations.


Another method is boiling, which is one of the simplest and most common sterilization techniques. In this method, the liquid is heated to 100°C, and many microorganisms are killed at this temperature. Boiling water at home is a good example of this process. It is an easy method that does not require special equipment. However, its disadvantage is that some resistant microorganisms—especially spore-forming bacteria—may not be completely eliminated through boiling alone.
